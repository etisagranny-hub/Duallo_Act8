01. PLASTIC POLLUTION

Plastic waste can enter the ocean through rivers, beaches, and waterways. Marine animals may swallow plastic or become trapped in discarded materials.

THE CHALLENGE: Reduce single-use plastics and dispose of waste properly.

02. CLIMATE CHANGE

Increasing ocean temperatures and changing ocean chemistry can affect coral reefs, marine habitats, and many ocean species.

THE CHALLENGE: Support responsible habits that help reduce environmental impact.

03. OVERFISHING

Overfishing occurs when fish are caught faster than populations can recover. This can disrupt food chains and affect marine ecosystems.

THE CHALLENGE: Encourage responsible fishing and sustainable seafood choices.

WHY IT MATTERS

  • Marine animals need healthy habitats.
  • Healthy oceans support biodiversity.
  • Ocean ecosystems benefit people worldwide.
