THE AMAZING OCEAN

  1. Oceans cover approximately 71 percent of Earth's surface.
  2. The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean on Earth.
  3. Oceans contain an enormous diversity of marine life.
  4. Coral reefs provide habitats for many species.
  5. Oceans help regulate Earth's climate.
  6. Some marine animals migrate thousands of kilometers.
  7. The Moon's gravity plays a major role in ocean tides.
  8. Much of the deep ocean remains unexplored.
  9. Phytoplankton contribute significantly to global oxygen production.
  10. Reducing pollution helps protect marine ecosystems.

THINK ABOUT THIS

The ocean is more than a beautiful view. It is a connected system that supports life, influences weather, and provides resources for people and wildlife.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Understanding the ocean helps us recognize why conservation is important and why our choices can make a difference.
