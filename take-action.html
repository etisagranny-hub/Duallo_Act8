FIVE WAYS TO HELP

01. Choose Reusable

Bring reusable bags, bottles, and containers instead of relying on unnecessary single-use plastics.

02. Dispose Responsibly

Keep rubbish out of streets, drains, rivers, and beaches.

03. Join Clean-Up Activities

Participate in community activities that remove litter from beaches and other natural areas.

04. Make Informed Choices

Learn about the environmental effects of the products you use and the food you consume.

05. Share Awareness

Encourage friends and family to learn about ocean protection and responsible waste management.

MY OCEAN PROMISE

I will make responsible choices, reduce unnecessary waste, respect marine life, and encourage others to help protect the ocean.
